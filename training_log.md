- **VO2max 41 → 44** over three weeks. The comeback block worked — fitness is trending up despite the disruption.
- **HRV baseline 34 → 36.** Sleep HRV dipped hard on several mornings (28 on 23 Aug, 29 on 1 & 8 Sep) but always recovered within a day.
- **12 runs in 20 days.** Consistency was better than it felt — the "wrecked fortnight" was mostly a perception problem.
- **Load never got out of hand.** Load ratio peaked at 0.94 (31 Aug) and otherwise sat 0.5–0.85. Training volume was not the problem.
- **Two things actually were the problem:**
  1. *Easy runs run at threshold.* 30 Aug "easy 8K" at HR 151; 2 Sep "easy 6K" at HR 141; the 27 Aug tempo was HR 152 — barely harder. Pace is being kept easy, effort is not. (Same pattern flagged on the 20 Aug 400s.)
