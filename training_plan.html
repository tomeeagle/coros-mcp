Cooper 1.5 mile — Avon wholetime

Track run, 6 laps, continuous. Pass = VO2 ≥ 42.3 ml/kg/min (~same bar as bleep 8.8). Target pace ~12 min total (~5:00/km avg). Warm up 10–15 min + 2–3 strides · even split — don't go out like a bleep test. Plan markers: Sat 22 Aug and Sat 12 Sep — log times and compare.

HIWFRS Chester (outdoor hill sub — no treadmill)

Avon WT uses Cooper 1.5 mile run, not Chester. This block is for HIWFRS + general aerobic base.
No gym access currently, so this runs as an outdoor hill walk instead of the treadmill protocol: find a steep hill (check gradient % on Strava/OS Maps/Komoot — need genuinely steep ground for 9–15% bands), hold ~9:41/km (6.2 kph) using the COROS pace alert, and break the climb into surges (~30s harder / 30s easier) rather than one flat effort, echoing the treadmill's gradient changes. No stopping mid-climb · RPE ≤18. If your local hills aren't steep enough, add a weighted rucksack — combine with the hill rather than using it alone. One or two real treadmill sessions before the actual HIWFRS test (leisure centre day pass, etc.) is worth it for pacing calibration, but isn't essential for fitness. Warm up · one programme per session · 3–10' cool-down. Progress bands: 9% → 12% → 15%. Tue + Fri from 4 Aug. Protocol from HIWFRS fitness advice — Programme 1 on Fridays; Programme 2 on Tuesdays.
